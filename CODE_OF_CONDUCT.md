# Code of conduct

ATLAS is a research tool, and research depends on honest, respectful
disagreement.

- Be respectful. Critique methods, data and code, not people.
- Be honest. Do not fabricate results, citations or test outcomes, and say
  plainly when something failed or is unknown.
- Be welcoming to newcomers and to people from every background.
- No harassment, personal attacks, discrimination or sexualised content in
  any project space.

Maintainers may remove content and restrict participation to uphold this
code. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
contact channels; reports are handled confidentially.
